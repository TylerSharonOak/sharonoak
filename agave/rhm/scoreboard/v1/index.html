Project Scoreboard

Placeholder name — not the final brand. Company: Agave Apps. Product name TBD.

An actionable, lightweight, local competitive scoreboard agencies can run again and again so that clients know where they stand.

Plain English: who actually matters, and which few levers move the scoreboard.

First buyer: agencies Status: hypothesis — want your reaction

Who it’s for

Digital / local agencies that sell and retain home-services (and similar) clients — and need a repeatable competitive picture without burning a strategist for days.

Owners may want this later. v1 proves the agency seat first.

What you get (v1 slice)

Slice Meaning
Search-first picture Paid, local, and organic vs a focused set — not 30 logos
Who matters Up to ~5 names that show up where it counts (channel mix can differ; start from search)
Overall standing Simple rank / score — one-glance “here’s the game”
Three levers Plain-English “pull these next,” not a channel encyclopedia

Example output shape (illustrative)

Your brand #6 overall · Search weak
Competitor A #1 · Local + organic
Competitor B #2 · Paid coverage

Levers: (1) local visibility · (2) organic gaps on money terms · (3) one paid test with a CPL cap — social stays out of v1.

Locked out of v1

Social — full suite across networks. Out for v1.

  • TAM / census essays
  • Agency sales close slides (your wrapper, not the product)
  • Anything that only works as a one-off custom build

Website is not locked out — you sell sites; you may want a thin website slice (not a full deep audit). See questions below.

Open decisions

Topic Question
Competitor list Do you (or the client) type competitors in, or should the product discover them (e.g. from search) with optional overrides?
Website in v1? If yes: what’s the minimum that still helps you sell/retain (forms / phone / book widget / speed?) vs a full audit?
Email capture If website is in: worth flagging whether they capture email / run a list? A lot of shops collect addresses and do nothing with them.

Scope watch

Search + website + email flags is already a lot. If website comes in, keep it to a handful of clear checks — not another full audit product.

Curious which of these matters most for you right now.

How it’s delivered

Branded HTML report — shareable link and/or PDF. Not PowerPoint.

You wrap it in Red Hook (or client) brand — logo, colors, domain. They get a clean page that works on a phone; PDF is print/save from the same thing. One artifact, not a slide deck that dies in email.

Later (v2 — not now)

  • Prospect reminders — nudges on the shared report until they opt out
  • Open alerts — ping the account person when a prospect or client opens the report (HTML links make this possible; PPT doesn’t)

How it might be bought

Per-report, small pack, or monthly seat for active clients — priced so it’s worth repeating, not a hero project every time. Exact number is a conversation.

Where I’d love your brain

If you could open a site, click a few buttons, some magic runs, and you get an output — what story would you want that output to help you tell (to a prospect or a client)?

That’s the main question. Outcome first — not “which slides from the old deck.” Whatever you name becomes the filter for what we build.
