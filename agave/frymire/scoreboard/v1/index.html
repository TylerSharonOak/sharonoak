Primary job: territory defense

Frymire has decades in North Texas. The risk isn’t “we don’t know our trade” — it’s someone else showing up first when a homeowner searches “AC repair Dallas,” “plumber Frisco,” or “emergency HVAC near me.”

This artifact is built to answer: who is encroaching, in which neighborhoods, on which services — before dispatch volume tells the story. That’s the fear-of-loss frame: defend ground you already earned.

HVAC and plumbing both matter. A competitor can win plumbing visibility in Collin County while Frymire still looks strong on HVAC in Dallas — or vice versa. The page has to work per service line and per location, not one blended “home services” score.

Who it’s for (this sprint)

Frymire Home Services leadership — internal review this week. Not for client or prospect distribution until scope and data are validated.

Frymire runs trucks, dispatch, and a sales process across the Metroplex (Dallas, Frisco, McKinney, Denton, Carrollton, and surrounding communities per frymire.com). This sprint tests whether a repeatable scoreboard helps defend those territories without a quarterly consultant deck.
