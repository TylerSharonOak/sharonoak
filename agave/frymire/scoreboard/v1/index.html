Project Scoreboard

Placeholder product name. Company: Agave Apps. This edition is framed for a home-services operator seat — not the agency edition shared with Red Hook.

A repeatable local competitive scoreboard for HVAC and home-services operators who need to know who actually wins the call — without a quarterly strategy project.

Plain English: who shows up when it matters, where you’re losing the game, and which few levers are worth pulling next.

Edition: operator / owner Market lens: DFW home services (illustrative) Status: sprint hypothesis — want your reaction

Who it’s for

Owners and GMs of established home-services shops — HVAC, plumbing, electrical — who compete locally for calls and booked jobs, not brand awards.

You already run trucks, dispatch, and a sales process. You don’t need another 40-page audit. You need a scoreboard you can rerun when a new competitor shows up, you open a territory, or marketing spend shifts.

Agencies get their own edition (see Red Hook draft). This sprint tests the operator seat.

What you get (v1 slice)

Slice Meaning
Search-first picture Local pack, paid, and organic for service + geo terms — not every trade directory
Who matters Up to ~5 names that actually take share in your service area
Overall standing Simple rank / score — “here’s the game” for your brand vs the field
Three levers Plain-English next moves: visibility, offer, conversion — not a channel bible
Thin website check Book-a-call path, phone prominence, speed — optional but useful for operators

Sprint scope watch

How it’s delivered

Branded HTML report — shareable link and/or PDF. Not PowerPoint.

Frymire (or any operator) brand on the wrapper — logo, colors, domain. Leadership gets a clean page on a phone; PDF is print/save from the same artifact.
