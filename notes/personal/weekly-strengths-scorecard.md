## Great week (strengths on)

| Strength | What it looks like in *your* job |
|---|---|
| **Command** | You made ownership and decisions explicit. People left conversations knowing who owns what, by when. You named real conflict (with boss prep when needed) instead of carrying ambiguity. You did not absorb other people’s risk work by default. |
| **Significance** | Most of your deep work was “only you can do this” — transformation altitude, standards, partnership model — not GL archaeology 100 people could do. You can point to visible impact (a decision landed, a standard reset, a pendulum nudge), not just fires survived. |
| **Futuristic** | You spent real time on the future picture (Christmas design/buy-in, Q1 delivery path, org rhythms). 1:1 with boss was forward-looking, not only status/history. You could say where the team is on the map. |
| **Strategic** | You protected ~4 hours for strategy/design (path-choosing, what *not* to do). You chose a clear route over reacting to every broken thing you dug up. Scope stayed tied to transformation outcome. |
| **Activator** | You started or claimed something concrete (mandate, design thread, accountability rhythm) — not just hinted. Momentum moved from talk → motion. Energy went into ignition, not martyr late nights. |

**Great week in one line:** You led transformation with clarity and forward motion, did high-altitude work, and still came home without fumes.

## Semi-objective scoring Rubric (0–2 each)

Score each theme **0 / 1 / 2** from evidence only (calendar, commits, emails, decisions) — not mood.

| Theme | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Command | No clear ownership calls; absorbed others’ work | Some clarity; one fuzzy handoff remained | ≥1 explicit ownership/decision reset; pushed real accountability |
| Significance | Mostly commodity/cleanup work | Mix; some high-altitude | Majority of deep work was only-you / transformation-visible |
| Futuristic | No forward artifacts | Mentioned future; little substance | Concrete forward artifact (plan, design, roadmap, offsite prep) advanced |
| Strategic | <1h path-choosing; pure react | 1–3h or weak prioritization | ≥4h strategy/design; clear “not doing” list |
| Activator | No start/claim; or only martyr motion | Small start; still mostly hints | Claimed or shipped a real next step in public with boss/partners |
| Life gates | Evenings lost to vigilance/guilt OR prayer commitment dropped hard | Partial: home mostly OK with slippage | Home present; after-hours check only on real ping; kept morning prayer if committed |

**Totals (max 12):**
- **10–12** — Great week (role fitting how you’re built)
- **7–9** — Mixed; note which themes starved
- **0–6** — Bad week (role using you wrong, or you drifted into fixer/martyr)
